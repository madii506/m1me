04 · how

    

      
1
mine
paste any coin's address, pick one that was born a minute ago, or bring your own image. it lands in a slot.

      
2
pair
put a second meme in the other slot and press pair. the lab splices the two pictures and the two names into one child.

      
3
launch
keep it or re-roll it, then press launch. the child goes to pump.fun with everything ready, and your wallet signs.

    

  


  

    
05 · questions

    

      
what does mining a meme mean?
picking a meme to work with. paste a solana coin's address and the lab reads its name, ticker and picture. or upload any image and name it yourself.

      
what happens when i pair two?
the lab makes a child: one picture spliced from both, a name made from both names, a ticker made from both tickers. four splices: splice, ghost, graft, mosaic. re-roll as much as you like.

      
how does launching work?
press launch and the child goes to pump.fun's launch page with its picture, name, ticker and line ready. your own wallet signs. m1me never holds keys or money.

      
does it cost anything?
the lab is free. pump.fun's own launch costs apply when you launch there.

      
is anything i upload stored?
no. pictures you upload never leave your browser. coin pictures are fetched through this site only so your browser can splice them.
